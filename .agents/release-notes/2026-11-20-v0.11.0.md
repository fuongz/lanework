## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-20

### Added

- **Hand off in the same conversation**: pick another agent's model in the Model menu and send. The chat goes on there, a "Context handoff" line marks the switch, and the sidebar keeps one row for it, with an icon for each agent it ran on. The new agent gets a short summary of the recent turns.
- **Conductor sessions stay the conductor's**: a session a conductor starts is read-only to you, with "Managed by" and a link back to the conductor, unless the conductor gives you access. The conductor answers that session's permission requests, and is told when one waits.
- **Conductor sessions above the prompt**: a conductor's chat lists the sessions it started, working or just finished, above the prompt.
- **Back to the conductor**: a session a conductor started shows Conductors, the conductor and the session in its breadcrumb.
- **Hover card on the sidebar**: rest on a session or conductor to see its branch, model and effort, permission mode, context used, tokens in and out, and estimated cost.
- **Preview tabs**: opening a chat shows it in one temporary tab, in italics, until you double click it (or its sidebar row) or send a message.
- **Load earlier turns**: a long chat opens on its latest turns and loads older ones as you go up. Long tool output has **Show full output**.
- **Usage limits that hold**: a limit on one Claude model (the weekly Opus or Sonnet cap) stops only that model's chats. Auto-continue survives a restart, and spending a banked Codex reset lifts Codex's limit at once.
- **Delete a project by its name**: deleting a project asks you to type its name first.

### Changed

- **Much lighter when idle**: Lanework no longer re-reads conversation transcripts in a loop. Idle CPU drops from most of a core to almost nothing, and memory from gigabytes to about a hundred megabytes.
- **Faster long chats**: transcripts are read only where they grew, streamed text is drawn once a frame, and other windows get a light status stream instead of every token.
- **Fewer idle agent processes**: at most three idle CLIs are kept warm; the oldest ends after five idle minutes and resumes on your next message.
- **Conductors ask less**: a conductor in Ask mode starts and reads its sessions without asking you; approving its sessions' permissions still asks.
- **Sub-agents, workers and background jobs** hang from their session in the sidebar on a curved guide.
- Conductor tabs show the conductor's name and icon.
- Deleting a session closes its tab.

### Fixed

- A message sent right after a handoff no longer stays on "Sending…" or disappears after a reload.
- A chat no longer shows "Working…" after hitting a usage limit.
- Handing off cancels the old chat's auto-continue, so two agents never pick up the same work.
- The breadcrumb no longer flashes "Sessions" before "Conductors".
